I'm not sure if anything's happening at Lawson for the feast of the Assumption, but it falls on a Friday this year. It's a Holy Day of Obligation and there's usually a midday Mass at Katoomba on Fridays. They're usually pretty good there, but have had a policy of only distributing Communion on the tongue to the last communicants. Not sure if that's still the case.

### 3rd August: 8th Sunday after Pentecost

* Be Thou My Vision
* Ave Verum
* O Bread of Heaven
* Help of Christians

### 10th August: 9th Sunday after Pentecost

* Mass XI, Credo I
