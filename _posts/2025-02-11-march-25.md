Have your Ave Regina Caelorum ready for any spare spaces at Mass.

 * Mass XVII, Credo I

 * When I Survey the Wondrous Cross
 * Attende Domine

 * God of Mercy and Compassion

### 23 March: 3rd Sunday of Lent

 * Mass XVII, Credo I
 * Audi Benigne
 * Lord Jesus Think on Me

 * My God, Accept my Heart this Day
 * Attende Domine
 * O Bread of Heaven
 * Come to Me all Ye who Labour
