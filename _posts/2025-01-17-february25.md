The short month - this year including Candlemas and Septuagesima

### 2/2 Candlemas / Purification of the Blessed Virgin Mary

- Mass IX, Credo 1
- Slavonic Sub Tuum
- Star of Ocean

### 9/2 5th Sunday after Epiphany

Last Sunday for Alleluias!

- Praise, my Soul
- Alleluia, dulce carmen - [here's my version](https://www.ccwatershed.org/2021/01/26/alleluia-dulce-carmen-sing-it-while-you-can/) but there are also [two versions here](https://societyofstbede.wordpress.com/2015/01/31/eve-of-septuagesima/) - not sure which is my favourite - what do you think?
- Let all mortal flesh
- All creatures of our God and King

### 16/2 Septuagesima

- Mass XI, Credo I
- Panis Angelicus - Casciolini or chant depending on who can sing
- Faith of our Fathers

### 23/2 Sexagesima

- Firmly I Believe
- Duo Seraphim
- O Bread of Heaven
- Hail Queen of Heaven
