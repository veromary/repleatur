May, the month of Mary, plus a continuation of the Easter season, which definitely hasn't finished yet.

### 2nd Sunday after Easter (4th May)

* Low Mass
* Hail Redeemer
* Ave Verum
* O Bread of Heaven
* Help of Christians

### 3rd Sunday after Easter (11th May)

* Sung Mass (Mass 1, Credo 1)
* Virgo Dei Genitrix
* Firmly I Believe

### 4th Sunday after Easter (18th May)

* Low Mass
* Praise to the Lord
* Veni Creator
* Soul of my Saviour
* Hail Queen of Heaven

### 5th Sunday after Easter (25th May)

* Sung Mass (Mass 1, Credo 1)
* Maria Mater Gratiae
* O Jesus Christ remember
