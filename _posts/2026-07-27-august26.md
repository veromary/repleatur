This is the month of the Immaculate Heart of Mary. It also includes the feast day of St Bernard of Clairvaux, so I'm hoping to sing Jesu Dulcis.

The feast of the Assumption on Saturday 15 August is a Holy Day of Obligation. We don't have a local Latin Mass that day, so you might like to plan ahead to go to Prospect 8am or 9.30am.

### 2nd August: 10th Sunday after Pentecost 

* Star of Ocean
* Ave verum
* Adoro te
* Jesus, my Lord, my God, my all

### 9th August: 11th Sunday after Pentecost 

* Sung Mass - Mass XI, Credo I
* Introit: Deus in loco sancto suo
* [Tua Jesu Dilectio, Palestrina][1]
* O Mother Mary of the Cross

### 16th August: 12th Sunday after Pentecost 

* Mary Immaculate - there's a fancy Bach tune, but not sure how we could put an organ accompaniment to it. Liebster Immanuel.
* Ave Maria - Arcadelt if we have voices, otherwise chant
* Ave Maris Stella ([Monteverdi's][2] would be awesome if we're up to it)
* Hail Queen of Heav'n

### 23rd August: 13th Sunday after Pentecost 

* Sung Mass - Mass XI, Credo I
* Introit: Respice, Domine,
* [Jesu Dulcis][3]
* Immaculate Mary

### 30th August: 14th Sunday after Pentecost 

* Firmly I believe
* [O bone Jesu][4]
* [Lauda Jerusalem][5]
* Help of Christians
