Second Half of the Easter Season, leading up to Pentecost!

### 4th Sunday after Easter (3rd May)

* Low Mass
* Praise to the Lord
* Veni Creator
* Soul of my Saviour
* Hail Queen of Heaven

### 5th Sunday after Easter (10th May)

* Sung Mass (Mass 1, Credo 1)
* Maria Mater Gratiae
* O Jesus Christ remember

### Sunday after Ascension (17th May)

* Low Mass
* Hail Queen of Heav'n
* O breathe on me, Breath of God
* [Godhead here in hiding](/pdf/hymns/Godhead-a4.pdf)
* Alleluia Sing to Jesus

### Pentecost (24th May)

* Sung Mass (Mass 1, Credo 1)
* Veni Creator all over the place - [maybe Couturier's 4 part version][1]
* Come Holy Ghost
