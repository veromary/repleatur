October is the Month of the Holy Rosary.

### 5th Oct: 17th Sunday after Pentecost

* Firmly I Believe
* [Ubi Caritas](https://newbookoldhymns.brandt.id.au/hymns/ubicaritas.html)
* O Bread of Heaven
* Hail Queen of Heav'n

### 12th Oct: External Solemnity of Our Lady of the Rosary

For anyone checking this page on a daily basis, you may have noticed some flip flopping between the 18th Sunday after Pentecost and Our Lady of the Rosary. Now it's been confirmed Our Lady of the Rosary, so I'm rolling it back again.

* [Mass IX, Credo I](https://bbloomf.github.io/jgabc/propers.html#ordinary=9#saint=Oct7#credo=344)
* Maria, Mater Gratiae
* We have a mad plan to learn Monteverdi's [Ave Maris Stella](/pdf/blackbook/ave-maris-monteverdi.pdf) but it's in 4 parts, but it is amazing. Or there's Ronan Reilly's [Magnificat](/pdf/blackbook/magnificat-reilly.pdf) which is 3 parts and quite singable.
* [Help of Christians](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VVV6LliL7Z0)
