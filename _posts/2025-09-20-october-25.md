October is the Month of the Holy Rosary.

### 5th Oct: 17th Sunday after Pentecost

* Firmly I Believe
* Ubi Caritas
* O Bread of Heaven
* Hail Queen of Heav'n

### 12th Oct: 18th Sunday after Pentecost

* Mass XI, Credo I
* Maria, Mater Gratiae
* God of Mercy and Compassion

### 19th Oct: 19th Sunday after Pentecost

* Praise to the Lord, the Almighty
* Ametur
* Jesus, my Lord, my God, my All
* O Purest of Creatures

### 26th Oct: Christus Rex

* Mass IV, Credo I
* Jesu Rex Admirabilis
* Lauda Jerusalem
* Hail Redeemer
