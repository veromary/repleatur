November is the month of the Holy Souls and also includes the First Sunday of Advent this year.

### 21st Sunday Post Pentecost - 2nd November

(All Souls day is transferred to Monday this year)

* Help, Lord the Souls that Thou hast Made, to the tune [Dundee](https://www.repleatur.net/pdf/organ/HelpLord-Dundee.pdf)
* [Panis Angelicus](/pdf/motets/Casciolini_Panis_Angelicus.pdf)
* [Christ be with me](https://hymnary.org/page/fetch/CPWI2010/423/high)
* Hail Queen of Heaven

### 22nd Sunday post Pentecost - 9th November

* Mass XI, Credo I
* Virgo Dei Genitrix
* O God, our help in ages past

### 23rd Sunday post Pentecost - 16th November

* Abide with me
* Ave Maris Stella
* Jesus, grant me this, I pray
* Crown Him with many Crowns

### Last Sunday post Pentecost - 23rd November

* Mass XI, Credo I
* Adoro Te
* O Jesus Christ Remember

### 1st Sunday of Advent - 30th November

* Hark a Herald Voice is Calling
* Alma Redemptoris
* Conditor Alme Siderum
* Come Thou Long Expected Jesus
