Month of the Precious Blood. Also, winter and the meeting room is still under construction.

As I'm writing this, there are plans to have weekly choir practice. Would people be able to come to Lawson for a choir practice in the church from 2pm?

**UPDATE:** Really cool little 3 part motet [Jesu, Salvator Mundi][1] by Menegali and paired with psalm toned verses by Catuí Côrte-Real Suarez on CPDL.org. Maybe we see if we can squeeze it in every Sunday in July in honour of the Precious Blood?

### 5th July: 6th Sunday after Pentecost

* Faith of our Fathers
* [Virgo Dei Genitrix](https://gregobase.selapa.net/chant.php?id=3320)
* Hail to thee True Body sprung, [sheetmusic](https://www.repleatur.net/pdf/hymns/hail-to-thee-CYS.pdf) - a tune from the Musica Sacra forum based on Ave Verum.
* Glory be to Jesus

### 12th July: 7th Sunday after Pentecost

* Sung Mass (Omnes gentes plaudite manibus) - changed to Low Mass due to coughs and colds
* Mass XI, Credo I
* [Jesu Rex Admirabilis](/pdf/motets/Jesu-Rex-chant-verses.pdf) - postponed
* Hail Redeemer
* [O Esca Viatorum][2] - starting on G worked really well.
* Soul of my Saviour
* O Purest of Creatures

### 19th July: 8th Sunday after Pentecost

* Praise, my soul, the King of Heaven
* [Maria Mater Gratiae](https://gregobase.selapa.net/chant.php?id=3030)
* O Bread of Heaven
* Holy God

### 26th July: 9th Sunday after Pentecost

* Sung Mass (Ecce Deus adjuvat me)
* Mass XI, Credo I
* [Panis Angelicus](/pdf/motets/Casciolini_Panis_Angelicus.pdf)
* Hail Queen of Heav'n
